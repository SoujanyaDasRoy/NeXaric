# NeXaric Design System

## Concept
A light-mode enterprise agency website. The aesthetic is modern, premium, trustworthy, and scalable without looking "startup flashy". Drawing inspiration from tier-1 enterprise platforms like Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Ramp, Retool, and Mercury.

## Color Palette

### Primary Brand Colors
- **Electric Enterprise Blue (Primary Blue):** `#2563EB`
  - *Purpose:* Main calls to action, prominent links, and primary brand indicators.
- **Bright Modern Blue (Secondary Blue):** `#4F7EFF`
  - *Purpose:* Accents, gradients, and secondary visual elements.
- **Brand Text / Headers (Deep Navy):** `#0A0F24`
  - *Purpose:* High contrast text for maximum readability and a premium feel.
- **Hover / Highlights (Accent Blue):** `#3B82F6`
  - *Purpose:* Interactive states for primary elements.

### Neutral UI Colors
- **Main Background (Soft White):** `#F8FAFC`
- **Card Background (Pure White):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Light Section Background (Cool Gray):** `#F1F5F9`
- **Border Color (Subtle Gray):** `#E2E8F0`
- **Muted Text (Slate Gray):** `#64748B`
- **Secondary Text (Medium Gray):** `#94A3B8`

### Enterprise Accent Colors
*Note: Use these very sparingly, primarily for functional feedback.*
- **Success (Emerald):** `#10B981`
- **Warning (Amber):** `#F59E0B`
- **Error (Soft Red):** `#EF4444`

## Component Styling

### Hero Section
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Heading:** `#0A0F24`
- **CTA Button:** `#2563EB`
- **Secondary Button Border:** `#E2E8F0`

### Cards
- **Style:** Clean white cards (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Border:** Subtle gray (`#E2E8F0`)
- **Hover State:** Slight blue-tinted shadow to indicate interactivity without being overbearing.

### Typography
- **Font Family:** Inter (Sans-serif)
- **Headings:** `#0A0F24` (Deep Navy)
- **Body Text:** `#64748B` (Slate Gray)

### Gradients
*Note: Use very subtle gradients only.*
```css
background: linear-gradient(135deg, #2563EB 0%, #4F7EFF 100%);
```
